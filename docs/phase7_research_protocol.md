# Phase 7A research protocol

## Freeze and objectives

Frozen on 2026-11-08 after input and coverage inspection and before computing age associations or fitting prediction models. The Phase 3 list is fixed by its [113-gene table](../reports/gene_overlap_genes.csv) and SHA-256. The input versions and checksums are in the [source manifest](../data/phase7_sources.json) and [HGNC manifest](../data/reference_provenance.json). This is an exploratory secondary analysis of public data.

The primary question is whether the 113-gene set carries a stronger, more reproducible donor-age expression signal than expression-matched genes in two human dermal fibroblast cohorts. The secondary question is whether a small Ridge model using the panel improves chronological-age prediction over constant and matched-panel baselines within discovery.

## Cohorts, identifiers and expression

- Discovery is [GSE113957](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE113957). Validation is [GSE226189](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE226189). Include healthy donors aged 22–89 inclusive, the common adult range. Keep one expression vector per source donor or cell line. Exclude all HGPS samples before association, feature filtering, matching or prediction. Require unambiguous GEO-to-expression joins, complete age and sex metadata, unique source donor proxies and finite, nonnegative expression. Stop on unresolved duplicate donors or expression profiles.
- Join discovery's first annotated gene symbol to an approved HGNC symbol, or to a unique previous symbol when an approved match is absent. Reject a conflict with a mapped RefSeq accession. Join validation's Ensembl gene IDs to the pinned HGNC Ensembl field after stripping any version suffix. Use Entrez IDs as the common key. Drop ambiguous and unmapped rows and all rows that collide on the same Entrez ID within a cohort. Keep a coverage ledger for all 113 panel genes. The rule uses a single pinned HGNC snapshot with no individual gene overrides.
- Use natural log of 1 plus submitted FPKM in discovery. In validation use natural log of 1 plus counts per million, with each denominator computed over all submitted gene-count rows before mapping. Never pool the transformed matrices. For associations, require positive expression in at least 20% of included donors and nonzero range in each cohort. For prediction, start with genes mapped in both cohorts without using validation expression and apply detection using discovery training donors only. Run the quantitative analysis if at least 80 panel genes pass joint association coverage and each cohort has at least 50 eligible independent samples.
- Use sex and discovery sequencing platform as association covariates. Validation has one platform and no per-donor batch or passage field in the GEO series matrix. The GR labels in validation titles describe non-overlapping or nearly non-overlapping age groups and are not a verified technical batch variable. Discovery ethnicity is sparse or unknown across some groups. Neither cohort supplies a common, complete set of additional covariates for this analysis.

## Association and replication

Use continuous age in years. Residualize log expression and age on an intercept, sex and, in discovery, platform. Estimate gene-wise partial Pearson correlations and two-sided t-test p-values. Apply Benjamini–Hochberg within the tested panel separately by cohort. Report how many genes meet q < 0.05 in both cohorts with concordant direction as a descriptive screen, without assigning a joint FDR. Report a discovery sensitivity estimate restricted to GPL18573 with the same panel.

The primary replication score is the mean product of discovery and validation partial correlations across jointly eligible panel genes. Positive products contribute evidence of aligned directions and larger magnitudes. Also report sign agreement among genes with nonzero effects in both cohorts and mean squared partial correlation in each cohort. Use two complementary comparisons.

1. Construct 1,000 control panels from jointly eligible genes outside all 113 original IDs. Match each target on within-cohort ranks of mean transformed expression and detection fraction, giving four equally weighted coordinates. Sample from the 32 nearest control candidates with Euclidean distance, in randomized target order and without duplicate controls within a panel. Report the target statistic, the control median and 2.5th–97.5th percentiles, the target's strict percentile and mean matching distances. The random panels provide a descriptive competitive reference. They do not guarantee a matched co-expression structure.
2. Hold the discovery effects fixed and permute validation ages within sex 9,999 times. Compare the observed replication score with the upper tail using the plus-one rule. Permuting donors jointly across genes preserves their expression correlations under the within-sex exchangeability assumption. This tests validation alignment, not enrichment against other genes. Report a percentile interval for the score from 1,000 independent donor bootstrap draws per cohort, stratified by sex and platform. Treat joint eligibility and the panel as fixed. The exploratory decision rule requires the score to exceed the matched 95th percentile and the one-sided validation permutation p-value to be at most 0.05. The 95th percentile is a descriptive cutoff, not a calibrated competitive test.

## Prediction

Evaluate discovery only. Use one shuffled five-fold split of eligible donor proxies. Fit all scaling and detection filters on the training donors and reuse identical held-out donors for each comparator. Train Ridge with an intercept and fixed alpha equal to the number of included features after training-only detection. There is no hyperparameter search. Compare the panel against training mean and median age and 40 Ridge control panels per fold. Match those panels on training-only mean transformed expression and detection ranks using the same nearest-control rule. Use the same fold assignments and penalty rule for control panels. As a diagnostic, include an ordinary least squares baseline with sex and platform only.

Report out-of-fold MAE, median absolute error, RMSE and R². MAE is primary. Report the median and 2.5th–97.5th percentiles of the 40 matched-panel MAEs and the fraction strictly worse than the target. Use 2,000 paired donor bootstrap draws of the out-of-fold absolute losses for the MAE difference from the median-age predictor. This conditional interval treats the held-out predictions as fixed and does not capture uncertainty from retraining or choosing a new cohort.

Use seed 20261108 with independent random streams for analysis steps. No external age predictions are planned. The first cohort summarizes RefSeq representative-isoform FPKM and the second uses gene-level read counts with a different annotation and library protocol. The data lack common technical controls or a prespecified calibrated transfer model. Independent validation is at the level of separately estimated age associations. Prediction concerns chronological age within one cohort and does not establish biological aging or lifespan prediction.

## Future evidence assistant

Build the first assistant around a deterministic evidence lookup, with language generation added after evaluation. Use Entrez and HGNC IDs to join the Phase 7 association table, the Phase 5 CellAge evidence matrix and the Phase 4 GO annotation table. Store each numerical claim with its analysis ID, cohort accession, eligible donor count, effect, uncertainty, coverage status and exact result row. Store experimental claims with CellAge record and DOI or PMID. Label GO evidence and reported experimental effects separately from association estimates.

For each answer return the specific rows and paper citations that support each claim. Distinguish replicated association, single-cohort result, direct perturbation evidence and missing evidence. Abstain when the required cohort, contrast, mapped gene or assay is absent. Before adding an interface, have a human verify a small held-out set of answerable and unanswerable questions covering replication, conflicting directions, missing genes and CellAge evidence. Score numerical agreement, citation entailment, evidence-type labeling and correct abstention. This phase trains no language model.
