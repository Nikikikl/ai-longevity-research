# Phase 7D evidence expansion for FOXO3 and SIRT6

Do curated aging and senescence claims for these genes reflect the experiments that support them, and how do those claims relate to the Phase 7A RNA-seq results?

Both genes have confirmed GenAge–CellAge overlap. We verified both HAGR records against the pinned archives and traced selected GO claims to the pinned GAF and primary papers. GenAge Build 21 provides an inclusion category in its export. The live entries give additional citations, which we use as context and keep separate from the archived rows. The GO comparison is limited to selected direct, positive Phase 4 annotations. ## FOXO3

**Cell culture.** Kim et al. used stable siRNA to reduce the forkhead transcription factor FOXO3a in primary human dermal fibroblasts. The abstract reports longer population doubling times, altered morphology, more SA-β-gal staining and reactive oxygen species, and increased p53 and p21 protein. These changes support an inhibitory role for endogenous FOXO3 in this culture model. The accessible evidence comes from loss of function. The CellAge `Unclear` subtype is appropriate at this review depth. Stable arrest, the precise trigger and the effect of activating FOXO3 remain unresolved by the abstract. The retained Phase 4 FOXO3 rows contain neither this PMID nor an exact direct annotation for cellular senescence (GO:0090398) or replicative senescence (GO:0090399). [Kim et al., 2005, abstract, PMID 15741276](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15741276/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/60.1.4) · [Phase 4 annotations](go_gene_annotations.csv)

**Human longevity.** The live GenAge entry links to Willcox et al. for the Japanese ancestry result. In a nested case-control study of Japanese American men, the investigators genotyped 213 men who reached at least 95 and 402 controls who died by age 81. For rs2802292, the reported odds ratio for the long-lived case group comparing GG with TT was 2.75 (95% CI 1.51–5.02). The human link is supported as a genotype association in this population. The study estimates odds of case status from genotype and leaves the consequences of altering FOXO3 expression untested. The archive also records model-organism support as an inclusion category. This review checked the cited human link and did not reassess each model claim. [GenAge entry 123](https://genomics.senescence.info/genes/entry.php?hgnc=FOXO3) · [Willcox et al., 2008, full text, PMID 18765803](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2544566/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0801030105)

## SIRT6

**Cell culture and citation reuse.** Nagai et al. depleted the chromatin-associated sirtuin SIRT6 by siRNA in cultured human knee chondrocytes. The journal abstract reports increased SA-β-gal positivity, p16 and DNA damage markers, with reduced proliferation. The author's doctoral methods/results summary specifies a 48-hour SA-β-gal endpoint and a short-term CCK-8 proliferation readout. The reviewed material supports premature senescence-associated changes after depletion. The CellAge `Replicative` label is more specific than the verified design. The available methods do not establish serial-passage exhaustion or arrest after the perturbation ends. The published full text was unavailable for this review. [Nagai et al., 2015, PMID 25819580](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25819580/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joca.2015.03.024) · [Kobe University doctoral summary](https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/D1006529/D1006529yy.pdf)

Nagai is also the cited source for positive regulation of telomere maintenance (GO:0032206, GAF 591460) and positive regulation of chondrocyte proliferation (GO:1902732, GAF 591502), both IMP. More telomere dysfunction-induced foci support an effect on telomere integrity. They do not establish that telomere length was maintained across passages. The CCK-8 result supports the proliferation claim as a proxy in this model. These are different readouts from one study alongside the CellAge senescence claim. [Nagai et al., abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25819580/) · [Kobe University doctoral summary](https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/D1006529/D1006529yy.pdf)

**Human age at death.** GAF 591445 assigns `determination of adult lifespan` (GO:0008340, IMP) to human SIRT6 and cites TenNapel et al. The paper compared naturally occurring rs107251 genotypes in the Iowa 65+ Rural Health Study. Adjusted mean age at death for the ten TT participants was 5.5 years lower than CC and 5.9 years lower than CT. The variant association is a real result, but the design did not establish a functional effect of the variant, perturb SIRT6 or show that the gene determines lifespan. The curated term and code remain in the ledger alongside this evidence boundary. [TenNapel et al., 2014, full text, PMID 25541994, Methods and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0115616) · [GO evidence code guide](https://geneontology.org/docs/guide-go-evidence-codes/)

**Mouse endpoints.** Kanfi et al., cited by live GenAge entry 239, reported that transgenic mice overexpressing Sirt6 lived longer than wild-type controls in males, with no significant extension in females in that study. This directly supports the scoped mammalian rationale for the entry. The available abstract supports the sex-specific result for that transgenic model. [GenAge entry 239](https://genomics.senescence.info/genes/entry.php?id=0239) · [Kanfi et al., 2012, abstract, PMID 22367546](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22367546/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10815)

GAF 591513 (GO:2000773, IMP, negative regulation of cellular senescence) cites Ghosh et al. Mouse embryonic fibroblasts from Sirt6-null animals showed more SA-β-gal and flattened morphology at passage 6 than wild-type cells. Reducing Trp53 dosage in the Sirt6-null background reduced those phenotypes. A separate survival endpoint in the same paper showed longer survival of compound mutant mice than Sirt6-null controls. The animal intervention here changes Trp53 on a Sirt6-null background. GAF 133834 for TP53 cites this same study, which also supplies the cellular phenotype comparison. [Ghosh et al., 2018, full text, PMID 29474172, Figures 1 and 2](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.32127) · [Evidence audit](phase7b_evidence_audit.md#phase-7d-extension)

## Comparison with Phase 7A

Phase 7A estimated associations of cultured fibroblast RNA expression with donor chronological age in 97 discovery donors (GSE113957) and 82 validation donors (GSE226189). Both genes were eligible in both cohorts. Values below are rounded from the unchanged association table.

| Gene | Discovery partial r | Discovery p | Discovery q | Validation partial r | Validation p | Validation q |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FOXO3 | +0.159 | 0.12358 | 0.19242 | −0.079 | 0.48588 | 0.89090 |
| SIRT6 | −0.307 | 0.00250 | 0.01050 | −0.075 | 0.50682 | 0.89090 |

FOXO3 changed sign and did not pass the within-cohort q threshold in either cohort. SIRT6 had a negative estimate in both cohorts, with a weaker estimate in validation and q < 0.05 only in discovery. Neither passed the Phase 7A cross-cohort gene screen. FOXO3's CellAge model is also dermal fibroblasts, but it measures the response to imposed knockdown. Nagai used chondrocytes and measured cellular changes after SIRT6 depletion. The human genotype studies measure survival-related endpoints, while the mouse studies measure experimentally altered survival or cellular phenotypes. None tests the same RNA-expression and donor-age association as Phase 7A. The estimates therefore neither confirm nor contradict the reported intervention effects. [Phase 7A association CSV](phase7_gene_associations.csv) · [Phase 7A results](phase7_baseline_results.md#independent-association-analysis)

The clearest follow-up is to ask whether the acute SIRT6 chondrocyte phenotype persists after the perturbation and whether it changes the number of serial doublings before stable arrest. Those endpoints address the gap behind the CellAge subtype and distinguish it from telomere damage. This finding concerns the two selected genes and supplies no estimate of how often curation has similar gaps.
